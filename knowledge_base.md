# Harbour Threads Staff Handbook (fictional company, sample data)

## Returns policy
Customers can return full-price items within 30 days of purchase with a receipt or proof of purchase. Items must be unworn, with original tags attached. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

## Sale item returns
Items bought on sale can be exchanged or refunded as store credit within 14 days. Sale items are not eligible for a cash refund unless they are faulty.

## Faulty items
If an item is faulty, customers are entitled to a repair, replacement or refund under Australian Consumer Law, regardless of whether it was on sale. Staff should log faulty items in the damage register before processing the refund.

## Online orders
Online orders can be returned in store or by post using the prepaid label in the parcel. Click-and-collect orders are held at the store for 7 days before being returned to the warehouse.

## Lay-by
Lay-by requires a 20% deposit and must be paid in full within 8 weeks. A $5 cancellation fee applies if the customer cancels the lay-by.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are valid for 3 years from the date of purchase. They cannot be exchanged for cash and can be used in store and online.

## Loyalty program
Members of the Harbour Club earn 1 point per dollar spent. Every 200 points converts into a $10 reward voucher, which expires 90 days after it is issued.

## Opening hours
Stores open 9am to 5:30pm Monday to Wednesday, 9am to 9pm on Thursday late-night trading, 9am to 6pm Friday and Saturday, and 10am to 5pm on Sunday.

## Staff discount
Team members receive a 30% discount on full-price items and an additional 10% off sale items after completing their probation period of 3 months.

## Rostering
Rosters are published every Wednesday for the following fortnight. Shift swaps must be approved by the store manager at least 24 hours before the shift.

## Cash handling
Tills are counted at the start and end of each shift. Any variance over $10 must be reported to the store manager and recorded in the till variance log.
